1. Overview

Young Grasshopper LLC ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. It applies to visitors and customers of our website, our AI Employee products and services, and anyone who communicates with us by email, phone, or text message.

By using our website or services, or by providing your contact information to us, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to operate our services and respond to you:

  • Contact information you provide, such as your name, business name, email address, and phone number.
  • Business details you share about your business, workflows, and needs so we can build and configure your AI Employee.
  • Messages you send us through our contact form, live chat, email, or text messaging.
  • Usage data such as pages visited and how you interact with our website, collected through analytics (see Section 8).

We do not sell your personal information.

3. SMS & Text Messaging Terms

When You Receive Text Messages From Us

When you opt in to receive text messages from Young Grasshopper — for example, to receive service updates, appointment or job reminders, alerts, or to interact with an AI Employee by text — we will only send text messages to the mobile number you provided and agreed to use.

How you opt in: By providing your mobile number and consenting (such as checking a consent box, texting a keyword like START, or agreeing on a call or form), you confirm that the number is yours and you authorize Young Grasshopper to send you text messages as described.

Message frequency: Message frequency varies. You may receive only the messages needed for the specific service or reminder you requested. We will not send you more messages than your selected service reasonably requires.

Message & data rates: Message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier. You are responsible for any carrier charges.

To opt out (STOP): You may opt out of receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP (or QUIT, END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE) to any message you receive. After you opt out, you will receive one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and no further marketing or reminder messages will be sent unless you re-opt in.

Need help (HELP): For help, reply HELP to any message, email hello@younggrasshopper.io, or call us and we will assist you.

Your number: Your consent is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services. We treat your mobile number as confidential and use it only for the texting program you joined.

Opt-in example script. When you agree to receive texts, you may be shown wording similar to:

Example Consent Disclosure

"By providing your mobile number and checking this box, you agree to receive text messages (including service and reminder messages) from Young Grasshopper at the number provided. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, reply STOP to cancel at any time. Consent is not a condition of purchase. View our Privacy Policy at younggrasshopper.io/privacy."

Text messages are delivered using a third-party messaging provider (currently Telnyx). We share only the data needed to deliver your messages, as described in Section 5.

We do not sell your information. We share information only with trusted service providers who help us operate, such as:

These providers may access personal information only as needed to perform their functions and are bound by confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information when required by law, in response to a legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Young Grasshopper, our customers, or the public.

6. Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to provide our services, or as required by law. When your data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

7. Your Choices & Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@younggrasshopper.io. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe. You may also unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email we send.

8. Cookies & Analytics

We currently do not respond to "Do Not Track" signals.
